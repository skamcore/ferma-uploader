Ferma uploader

A private tool for managing my own YouTube channels.

Ferma uploader is a personal command-line tool. It is used by a single person (the developer) to upload short videos to YouTube channels that the developer owns, with a scheduled publish time, and to read view statistics of those videos.

It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, no other users, and no data of other people is collected.

What it does with Google / YouTube access

  • Uploads videos to the developer's own channels (YouTube Data API, scope youtube.upload).
  • Reads public statistics (views) of the developer's own published videos.
